ParentPeak is your warm companion through everyday family life – calm, clear and all in one place.

You don't have to be perfect. Just be there. ParentPeak brings together exactly what parents really need – without the feature jungle.

WHAT PARENTPEAK CAN DO

• Events & Activities: Real family events near you – location-based, with distance display. Theatre, classes, playground meetups.
• Giveaway Market: Give away children's items instead of throwing them out. Local and in solidarity. Parents helping parents.
• AI Parenting Guidance: A sound piece of advice in 10 seconds, based on nonviolent communication (Rosenberg, Hüther, Montessori and more).
• Impulses & Development: Daily parenting impulses, a development check for every age and an expert library.
• Calm & Rituals: Gentle evening rituals, a quiet timer, a bedtime story and a weekly review – moments to breathe.
• Family Calendar: Appointments, holidays and school breaks at a glance, with smart reminders.
• Family Kitchen: Age-appropriate recipes in one tap, with shopping list and kids' favourites.
• Family Community: Discover families near you, share moments and chat directly – connection instead of isolation.
• Family Finance: Child benefit, housing benefit and more – which support are you entitled to? Your guide.
• Family Hub: Shopping list, to-dos and a child profile. Organisation without the stress.

OUR PROMISE

• Privacy first: as little data as possible, no tracking, no advertising profiles.
• No photos of children: in the community you share illustrations and moments, never your children's faces.
• Safe and moderated: reporting and blocking are available everywhere.
• AI transparency: where AI is used, we show it. AI answers do not replace professional advice.

Relationship before discipline. Trust instead of control. Being there instead of being perfect.

Through everyday family life, together.
