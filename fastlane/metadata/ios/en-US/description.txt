ParentPeak is your warm companion through everyday family life – calm, organised and all in one place.

From weekly planning to shared meals to connecting with other families: ParentPeak brings together the things that make life with children easier.

WHAT TO EXPECT

• Family Hub: shopping list, to-dos and a child profile (sizes, allergies, doctor, check-ups) – all in one place.
• Calendar: the whole family's appointments at a glance, with reminders.
• Family Kitchen: recipe ideas that match your children's ages, seasonal and practical, linked to your shopping list.
• Impulses & Development: gentle daily impulses, a parent knowledge area and an age-appropriate development check.
• Family Community: meet other families nearby, connect and share moments from your family life – safe and moderated, without photos of children.
• Events & Activities: find events and outings in your area.
• AI Parenting Guidance: caring support for everyday questions, based on nonviolent communication.
• Family Finance: an overview of fixed costs and a guide to family benefits.

OUR PROMISE

• No ads, no tracking, no sharing your data for advertising.
• No photos of children: in the community you share illustrations and moments, never your children's faces.
• Safe and moderated: reporting and blocking are available everywhere.

ParentPeak is free to use. We keep developing the app together with parents.

Through everyday family life, together.
